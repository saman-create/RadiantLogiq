## Platform

web

## Users

RadiantLogiq primarily serves healthcare organizations and the clinical, operational, and IT leaders who evaluate imaging and workflow infrastructure. Clinicians are the end users of its clinical reasoning and healthcare workflow products.

## Product Purpose

RadiantLogiq provides deterministic clinical reasoning and healthcare workflow infrastructure for imaging decisions. It exists to help healthcare organizations secure, automate, and optimize clinical decision loops through an interoperable suite spanning clinical decision support, electronic health records, telehealth, imaging review, and e-prescribing.

Success means organizations can evaluate and adopt the relevant RadiantLogiq products while retaining transparent, repeatable logic, an auditable decision trail, and the product's stated patient-data handling constraints.

## Positioning

RadiantLogiq differentiates itself through deterministic, auditable clinical reasoning rather than probabilistic or hallucination-prone decision logic. Its stated architecture processes patient variables in memory during the encounter and does not persist them beyond the encounter context, forming its zero-PHI-footprint position.

## Operating Context

The product is evaluated and used within healthcare environments that depend on EMR/EHR, PACS, clinical decision support, virtual-care, identity-verification, messaging, payment, and e-prescribing systems. Existing product workflows include imaging review and routing, appropriateness logic, clinical notes, virtual visits, scheduling and waitlists, patient portals, and e-prescribing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product suite consists of RadiantLogiq TeleHealth, RadiantLogiq PACS, RadiantLogiq CDS, RadiantLogiq EHR, and RadiantLogiq MEDS.
- The enterprise suite combines CDS, EHR, and TeleHealth through a single deterministic data loop and includes the capabilities recorded in `src/content.ts`.
- Existing navigation and product destinations are durable functionality and must be preserved unless explicitly changed.
- Existing metrics, integrations, compatibility statements, compliance statements, and program memberships may be used only as recorded in verified project content.
- Do not invent testimonials, customers, certifications, statistics, partnerships, pricing, clinical outcomes, or other evidence.
- RadiantLogiq MEDS remains presented as a waitlist product.
- The current project is a React and TypeScript single-page landing site built with Vite. Linked product, pricing, company, resource, authentication, and legal destinations are not implemented by this project.

## Brand Commitments

## Product Principles

1. Prefer deterministic, exact, repeatable clinical logic over probabilistic decision-making.
2. Make clinical reasoning transparent and auditable.
3. Minimize patient-data persistence and preserve the stated zero-PHI-footprint architecture.
4. Keep the platform modular and interoperable with healthcare systems already in use.
5. Support product evaluation with verified evidence and never fabricated proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must remain responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop; keyboard operable; semantically structured; visibly focused; and usable with reduced motion. Product copy and interface compositions must not expose real patient information.
